Customers who take meat now and pay later (deni). Sell to them with Charge → Credit, then record each weekly payment here. Red Overdue means no payment for more than 7 days.

Shop settings

Branch & sync

Choose the branch this till is at. With sync on, every sale, payment, expense and stock-in is sent to the online database, and the owner's laptop can see all branches in Sales. No internet? Sales wait and send later.

Receipt details

These print at the top and bottom of every receipt.

Fresh, unprocessed meat is usually VAT-exempt in Kenya, so VAT starts at 0%. Check with KRA or your accountant.

Staff

Everyone who uses the till. Each sale and the end-of-day report shows who served.

Activity log

Every important action: who changed prices, deleted products, gave refunds or discounts, cleared a sale, received money, and who used the till. It cannot be edited.

Customer privacy

The till keeps customer names and phone numbers. Show or send this notice to customers, and only save a phone number when the customer agrees.
